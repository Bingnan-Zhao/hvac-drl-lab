HVAC 3D viewer - Case 05 demo
=============================

Double-click HVAC_Case05_Demo.html. It opens in any recent browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).
Nothing to install and no internet connection needed: the 3D model, the viewer and the
recorded simulation data are all inside this one file.

- By default it plays back real runs of the Modelica plant model (two summer days and two
  winter days, each under G36 reset, a fixed 13 degC set point and the DRL agent).
  Switch period and strategy in the panel on the right.
- If you change a zone set point, internal gains, outdoor air or the AHU switch, the page
  switches to a simplified in-browser model (yellow badge, top left). Its numbers are
  approximate. "Back to recorded" returns to the real run.
- Mouse: left-drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom. Click a unit, then drag
  Explode to take it apart.

Phone version: HVAC_Case05_Demo_mobile.html (same data; controls in a bottom sheet -
tap "Controls" - and the view fits a portrait screen).
